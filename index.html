Structural results

Theorems of the FieldSpace index

These are combinatorial results and file-audit properties. They do not assert that the field equations describe nature.

Theorem 1

Triplet completeness

Every unordered three-sector subset of the 11-sector space occurs exactly once.

|T| = C(11,3) = 165

Reason: choosing 3 distinct sectors from 11 without order gives 165 combinations.

Theorem 2

Uniform sector participation

Each sector appears in exactly 45 triplets.

deg(v) = C(10,2) = 45

Reason: fix one sector and choose the remaining two from the other ten.

Theorem 3

Uniform pair participation

Every unordered pair of sectors appears together in exactly 9 triplets.

λ₂ = 11 − 2 = 9

Reason: after fixing a pair, any of the remaining nine sectors can be the third member.

Equation families

Relevant laws represented in the source

These names identify established mathematical or physical equation families used by the formulation. They should not be read as validation of the new cross-sector couplings.

01

Einstein field equation family

Triplets containing E use an Einstein-tensor equation of the form Gμν + Λgμν = κ(…), with scalar, gauge, and mixed interaction stress terms.

Appears in 45 triplets.

02

M-sector gauge field equation

Triplets containing M include a covariant gauge-divergence equation with channel-dependent currents and mixed interaction terms.

Maxwell-like / gauge-field structure; exact index notation in the source requires normalization review.

03

M-sector Bianchi identity

The source pairs the M gauge equation with an antisymmetrized derivative identity, corresponding in conventional notation to the electromagnetic Bianchi identity.

A geometric identity for a field strength when the standard interpretation applies.

04

S-sector gauge field equation

Triplets containing S use a second gauge-type field equation with currents and mixed terms. The source calls this the symmetry gauge sector.

Its exact gauge group and transformation law are not specified in the source.

05

S-sector Bianchi identity

The S field strength is likewise accompanied by an antisymmetrized derivative identity.

Formal interpretation depends on the eventual definition of the S gauge field.

06

Covariant Klein–Gordon-type scalar law

Scalar sectors use equations built around □g φX + mX²φX, augmented by curvature and cross-sector couplings.

There are 360 scalar equations across the full triplet catalog.

07

Non-minimal curvature coupling

Scalar equations include terms of the form ξX R φX, a standard curved-spacetime scalar-field structure, alongside EFMW-specific phenomenological couplings.

Its interpretation in triplets without E is an explicit open question.

08

Mixed interaction stress

Gravitational blocks include Tμν(int) as a container for mixed interaction stress.

This is a source-defined placeholder, not yet a derived conservation law.
